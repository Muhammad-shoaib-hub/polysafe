The problem PolySafe is trying to solve

When a patient takes several medicines, the number of possible interacting pairs grows fast. A checker that just lists every alert still leaves the hard question unanswered: which one actually matters right now?

About PolySafe

PolySafe was developed through a collaboration between pharmacy and computer science, bringing together clinical knowledge and technical development.

The project was initiated with a central focus on polypharmacy — helping users identify, prioritize, and understand clinically important drug interactions when multiple medicines are involved.

PolySafe — Detect. Prioritize. Understand.
