      Why PolySafe, when checkers already exist?

      Most checkers answer

      "Does Drug A interact with Drug B?" — leaving you to sort through a flat list of alerts yourself.

      PolySafe is built around

      "Which interaction in this list matters most, why does it matter, and what should a pharmacist consider?"

      PolySafe is a prototype built on a deliberately limited, curated dataset — it is not a replacement for DrugBank, Micromedex, Medscape or a pharmacist's professional judgment. A pair with no record shown means "not yet reviewed," never "confirmed safe."

      PolySafe — a student project concept. For education and demonstration, not clinical use.
